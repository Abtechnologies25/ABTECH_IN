About Us

AB Technologies is an organization that primarily deals with developing research concepts and real time projects. AB Technologies is specialized in guiding the students to develop engineering projects with real time applications. AB Technologies assists the research scholars and mentor them in understanding the concepts, ideas and also support them in writing and publishing their research works in reputed journals of different standards such as UGC, Scopus indexed and SCI indexed journals. The organization is well versed in assisting Synopsis and Thesis development.

AB Technologies is also involved in conducting Training, Internship and Courses in various disciplines like Embedded Systems, Internet of Things (IoT), Robotics & Automation, Artificial Intelligence, Matlab, PCB, CAD design, Python, Java, VLSI, ANSYS, Antenna Design, Web Development, Game Development, Computer Hardware & Software, Database Management and networking.

AB Technologies is experienced in the preparation of proposals and in the implementation of engineering oriented funded projects. AB Technologies is skilled and engaged in the manufacturing of Lab Equipment. AB Technologies is well known for conducting various workshops, value added courses, trainings, and Guest Lecture Programs, National and International conferences in various colleges across India.
