About MoU

A Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) is a formal agreement that establishes a collaborative relationship between two organizations. It facilitates cooperation in various domains aligned with the objectives of the MoU, fostering mutual growth and creating shared benefits for both organizations.

AB Technologies is co-operatively working with different Organizations, Colleges and Universities by actively signing MoUs with them.
